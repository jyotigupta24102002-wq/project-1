Find your size with a coin

No tape measure needed. Use any Indian coin and your phone.

  1. Pick any Indian coin. Use the same coin for all 10 nails.
  2. Place the coin flat against one nail, touching the nail.
  3. Take a clear photo straight from above. Keep the whole coin in view.
  4. Repeat for each nail. One nail, one photo.
  5. Upload all 10 photos with your order. I will measure and make your set to fit.
